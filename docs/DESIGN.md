# Arcode visual identity: Ember and Chalk

This document defines how Arcode looks. It replaces the "Look and feel"
part of `docs/UI_SPEC.md`; the layout and behaviour there still apply.
Read it before changing any colour, size, radius or chrome component.

Themes: **Arcode Ember** (dark, the default dark theme) and **Arcode
Chalk** (light, the default light theme). The old Arcode Dark, Arcode
Light and Arcode Midnight stay selectable as "Classic" themes.

## 1. Concept

Arcode is a workbench. The window background, the **canvas**, is a
surface. The tools lie on it as separate rounded **panels** with 6 px of
canvas between them: the sidebar, the editor (with all its split panes)
and the bottom panel. The title bar, the icon rail and the footer have no
fill of their own and are drawn straight onto the canvas. There are no
full-width bands, no coloured status bar, no grey activity-bar strip and
no tab underlines.

One warm accent, **ember** (amber in Ember, amber-brown in Chalk), has
one rule: **ember marks where you are, plus the one primary action on a
surface**. Where you are means:

- the 1 px edge of the focused panel,
- the **ember mark** (a 7 px diamond) on the active tab of the focused
  pane, on the current call-stack frame and on the paused readout,
- the active rail icon, the selected palette row's icon, matched letters,
- the caret.

The single primary action is Continue while paused, Open Folder on the
welcome screen, or the confirm button of a dialog. **Run is not solid**
while idle: it is an outlined button with an ember play icon.
Everything else is warm neutrals plus the status colours.

The signature is the **spark**: the logo is the arc of a thrown spark
that lands as the ember diamond, and that same diamond follows your focus
around the window. A quieter second signature: every number the app
reports (Ln/Col, counts, exit codes, durations, memory, line numbers in
lists) is set in JetBrains Mono, like readings on an instrument.

### What Arcode must not look like

These rules exist so later changes do not drift back:

- **Not VS Code.** No full-width coloured status bar. No activity bar
  with its own fill and a left accent bar. No tab top or underline accent.
  No UPPERCASE section headers ("EXPLORER"). No blue chrome accent. No
  Dark+ / Light+ syntax colours.
- **Not JetBrains (including the "Islands" look) or Fleet.** Floating
  panels on a canvas are shared structure, so identity must come from
  the palette, the spark logo, the ember diamond, the mono readouts and
  the type. Do not add tool-window stripes or side tool-window buttons
  with text labels, do not use square-cornered tabs with a coloured
  bottom line, do not use Darcula orange keywords.
- **Not Zed, Sublime or Nova.** No blue-grey flat UI, no Monokai or
  Mariana syntax, no translucent sidebars.
- **Not Anthropic or Claude.** No orange rounded-square logo tile, no
  cream or kraft canvas with a clay or terracotta accent. Chalk's canvas
  is a neutral grey-sand and its accent is amber-brown.
- The syntax palette is checked against 18 dark and 9 light schemes in
  section 3.4; a change to it must keep "near roles" at 1 or less per scheme.

### 2.4 Colour rules

1. Status is never colour alone. Errors carry `circle-x`, warnings
   `triangle-alert`, info `info`, success `circle-check` or the play
   triangle. Error diagnostics are a 1 px **wavy** underline; warnings a
   1 px **straight** underline; info a straight underline in `info`; hints
   have no underline (gutter dot and lens only).
2. Deleted lines are always a **triangle** in the change lane, the
   overview ruler and diff views, never a bar. Breakpoints are circles;
   the ember mark is a **diamond**; Run is a triangle.
3. `diff_*` and `accent_alt` are for bars, rings and dots. When a git
   state is written as text (M/A/U/D letters, `~2 +1` in the title bar,
   rail badges), it uses `info` (modified), `success` (added, untracked)
   and `error` (deleted), which all pass 4.5:1.
4. Unsaved is a **hollow ring** (8 px, 1.5 px stroke, `accent_alt`), not a
   filled dot. "changed" in the debugger uses the same ring plus the word.
5. `text_disabled` is for disabled controls only. Ignored or excluded
   files (`target/`) use `text_muted` in italic with the icon in
   `text_disabled`.
6. Hover, selection, line highlight and the paused band are solid
   colours. Never draw `hover` as `#ffffff12` in new code.
7. The accent never signals status. A focused panel is framed in amber,
   errors are crimson; section 3.3 shows the separation.

## 4. Typography

**UI font:** Inter, bundled (OFL) as Regular (400), Medium (500) and
SemiBold (600), with the system UI font as fallback. Features `cv11`
(single-storey a) where GPUI applies them. **Code, terminal and every
readout:** JetBrains Mono (already bundled), ligatures off by default.
Readouts are in mono, so they never jitter as values change, even if
`tnum` is ignored by a platform's text system.

The scale is in whole pixels only (half sizes rasterise soft on 1x Linux
screens) and is relative to `ui.font_size` (default 13):

| step | size | weight | used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| micro | font_size - 2 = 11 | Inter 500 / Mono 400 | kbd chips, count badges (mono), rail badges (mono 10 is the one exception, inside a 14 px badge) |
| meta | font_size - 1 = 12 | Inter 500 (footer), 400 (meta), 600 (section headers) | footer, breadcrumbs, descriptions, section headers inside panels ("Call stack", "Breakpoints") |
| readout | 12 | JetBrains Mono 400 | Ln/Col, MB, counts, `exit 101 · 0.92 s`, `main.rs:11`, line columns in lists |
| body | font_size = 13 | Inter 400; 500 for active tab, selected row | tree rows, tabs, lists, menus, buttons, palette rows |
| title | font_size + 1 = 14 | Inter 600, letter-spacing -0.005em | panel titles ("Files", "Run and debug"), project name (-0.01em), settings group headings |
| input | font_size + 2 = 15 | Inter 400 | palette query |
| heading | 20 | Inter 600, -0.01em | settings page title |
| display | 28 | Inter 600, -0.02em | welcome title |

Rules: sentence case everywhere, never UPPERCASE labels; hierarchy comes
from weight and size, not letter-spacing or caps. Inline lenses, blame
and inline debug values use Inter 12, so they never read as code.

**Code:** 13 px on a 20 px line (1.54) in the mockups; the editor keeps
the user's `editor.font_size`. Line numbers 12 px. Comments italic.
**Terminal:** 12 px on 18 px. **Debug console:** 12 px on 17 px.

### 5.2 Elevation and low-end mode

Panels never have shadows. Only overlays do, through `Elevated`:

- `elevated()`: popovers, menus, tooltips, toasts: one layer, offset y 8,
  blur 16, colour `shadow`.
- `elevated_lg()`: palette and dialogs: one layer, offset y 12, blur 24,
  colour `shadow`. One layer only and blur 24 or less, to stay cheap on
  integrated GPUs.
- No backdrop dim behind the palette or dialogs (the first-run dialog and
  the tour keep their existing dim, which is a single solid quad).

**Low-end mode** (`ui.low_end_mode`): no shadows at all, no animations,
slower background refresh. Overlays keep their `border_strong` edge, so
they stay distinct. Everything else (panels, radii, gaps, colours) is
identical, because a rounded bordered rect is one SDF quad in GPUI and
costs the same as a square one. There are no gradients, blur, alpha
layers or continuous frames anywhere.

### 5.3 Rounded corners and GPUI content masks

GPUI content masks are axis-aligned rectangles, so a child painted edge
to edge (line highlight, paused band, selection, terminal selection)
would square off the 10 px panel corners. Rules:

- Lists and trees are inset chips (6 px), so they never touch the edges.
- The editor and terminal paint row bands 1 px in from the panel's left
  and right edges, and the scroll viewport has 4 px bottom padding.
- When the last visible row band touches the panel's bottom edge, paint it
  with bottom corner radii of 9 px (`paint_quad` takes `Corners`).
- Headers and tab strips occupy the top corners and have no fill of their
  own, so they need nothing.

## 7. Implementation plan

The code is GPUI 0.2.2 and needs nothing beyond its primitives: `div`
with `bg`, `border_1`, `rounded`, solid `Hsla`, `svg()`, text runs,
`UnderlineStyle { wavy }` and `paint_quad` with `Corners`. Order the work
so each step builds and passes tests on its own.

### Step 1: theme format (`crates/arcode-config`)

1. `src/theme/palette.rs`: add five keys to `UiColors` (`canvas`, `pill`,
   `border_strong`, `selection_inactive`, `paused_line`), each documented.
2. `src/theme/parse.rs`: the new keys are **derived** when a theme table
   does not set them, so old and third-party themes keep working and do
   not inherit Ember's values for keys their own colours should drive:
   - If the table sets the key: use it.
   - Else, if the table sets the source keys: derive from the theme's
     resolved colours: `canvas = title_bar`; `pill = surface_raised`;
     `border_strong = mix(text_muted, border, 0.5)`;
     `selection_inactive = line_highlight`;
     `paused_line = mix(warning, background, 0.14)` (opaque).
   - Else inherit from the base theme.
   Add `Color::mix(a, b, t)` in `src/theme/color.rs` if missing.
3. `themes/arcode-ember.toml` and `themes/arcode-chalk.toml` with the
   values in section 8 (complete: every `[ui]`, `[syntax]` and
   `[terminal]` key).
4. `themes/arcode-dark.toml`, `arcode-light.toml`, `arcode-midnight.toml`:
   add the five keys explicitly (values that match each classic look),
   since built-ins are parsed strictly.
5. `src/theme/registry.rs`: `BUILTIN_THEME_NAMES = ["Arcode Ember",
   "Arcode Chalk", "Arcode Dark", "Arcode Light", "Arcode Midnight"]`,
   sources to match; `builtin_base()` returns Ember / Chalk.
6. `src/config/sections.rs`: `UiSettings::default()` dark_theme "Arcode
   Ember", light_theme "Arcode Chalk". Configs that name "Arcode Dark"
   keep it. Update `assets/default-config.toml` comments and
   `src/theme/tests.rs` (built-in count, names, derivation tests:
   a table with only `title_bar` gets `canvas == title_bar`; a table with
   nothing gets the base's `canvas`).
7. Add `editor.inline_diagnostics` (`"errors"` default, `"all"`, `"off"`).

### Step 2: kit (`crates/arcode-ui-kit`)

1. `theme.rs`: add the five fields to `ThemeColors`, aliases

4. `icon.rs` and `assets/icons/`: add `logo-arc.svg` and `ember.svg`

   - `Tooltip`: `panel_raised`, `border_strong`, radius 6.

### Performance

Per frame this adds about six rounded quads (panel borders) and a few
7 px diamonds, and removes three full-width bar fills. No continuous

while an overlay is open, one layer each, and never in low-end mode.

## Kit components for the redesign

What `arcode-ui-kit` offers the shell, editor and terminal lanes (all
re-exported at the crate root; `use arcode_ui_kit::*` style imports work).
Functions that build a styled `Div` take `cx: &App` for the colours; the
`RenderOnce` builders read the theme when they render. Signatures are
abbreviated: `Str` is `impl Into<SharedString>`, `Id` is
`impl Into<ElementId>`, `Click` is `impl Fn(&ClickEvent, &mut Window,
&mut App) + 'static`.

Difference from section 7: `panel(focused)` and the other `Div` builders
take `cx`, because GPUI 0.2.2 has no theme-aware style refinements.

### Theme (`theme(cx).colors`)

- New fields `canvas`, `pill`, `border_strong`, `selection_inactive`,
  `paused_line` (theme keys), `shadow` (overlay shadow colour, derived
  from `is_dark`), `accent_hover` (solid Primary hover, `#f6b468` /
  `#74470f` for Ember / Chalk, derived from `accent`).
- `colors.panel()`: the panel fill (legacy `background`).
  `colors.panel_raised()`: overlays (legacy `surface_raised`).
- `colors.get(key)` / `ColorKey` know every new key.
- `shadow_color(is_dark) -> Hsla`, `accent_hover(accent, is_dark) -> Hsla`.
- Config side: `arcode_config::{DEFAULT_DARK_THEME, DEFAULT_LIGHT_THEME}`
  ("Arcode Ember", "Arcode Chalk"), `Color::mix(a, b, t)`,
  `InlineDiagnostics::{Errors, All, Off}` with `.shows(is_error)` for
  `settings(cx).editor.inline_diagnostics`.

### Metrics and type (`arcode_ui_kit::metrics`)

- Constants of 5.1: `GAP`, `TITLE_BAR_H`, `RAIL_W`, `FOOTER_H`,
  `PANEL_RADIUS`, `OVERLAY_RADIUS`, `POPOVER_RADIUS`, `CONTROL_H`,
  `CONTROL_H_PANEL` (26), `CONTROL_RADIUS`, `ICON_BUTTON_RADIUS`, `ROW_H`,
  `ROW_H_DENSE` (22), `ROW_H_PALETTE` (32), `ROW_H_WELCOME` (40),
  `ROW_INSET`, `ROW_RADIUS`, `TAB_STRIP_H`, `TAB_H`, `TAB_H_SMALL` (24),
  `RAIL_TILE`, `RAIL_TILE_RADIUS`, `RAIL_PITCH`, `SIDEBAR_W`, `BOTTOM_H`,
  `EMBER`, `PANEL_TITLE_H` (40), `SECTION_H` (30), all `Pixels`.
- `metrics::icon_size::{RAIL, ROW, TOOLBAR, INLINE}`: 18 / 15 / 14 / 13.
- `WEIGHT_REGULAR`, `WEIGHT_MEDIUM`, `WEIGHT_SEMIBOLD`: Inter 400 / 500 /
  600 as `FontWeight`.
- `type_scale(cx) -> TypeScale { micro, meta, readout, body, title, input,
  heading, display }`: 11 / 12 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 20 / 28 at
  `ui.font_size = 13`, whole pixels. `TypeScale::new(font_size)`.

### Fonts and icons

- `BUNDLED_UI_FONT` ("Inter", Regular, Medium and SemiBold bundled),
  `BUNDLED_MONO_FONT` ("JetBrains Mono"), `SYSTEM_UI_FONT`
  (".SystemUIFont"; the UI font picker offers it as "System UI Font" and
  names the unset default "Inter").
- `ui_font_family(cx) -> SharedString`: `ui.font_family`, else Inter, else
  the system UI font when Inter failed to load. The workspace root already
  uses it; set it on any other root (tooltips do it themselves).
- `mono_font_family() -> SharedString`: the font of every readout.
- `IconName::LogoArc` (the logo arc) and `IconName::Ember` (the diamond).
  All Lucide icons now use `stroke-width="1.75"`.
- `icon(name).group_hover_color(group, color)`: an SVG does not inherit
  the parent's text colour, so hover tints go on the icon itself.

### Effects

- `.elevated(cx)`: one shadow, y 8, blur 16, `colors.shadow` (popovers,
  menus, tooltips, toasts). `.elevated_lg(cx)`: y 12, blur 24 (palette,
  dialogs). Both draw nothing in low-end mode.

### Surfaces (`components/surface.rs`)

- `panel(focused: bool, cx) -> Div`: `panel` fill, 1 px `border` or
  `border_focused`, radius 10, `overflow_hidden`, flex column.
- `panel_border(focused, cx) -> Hsla`: just the edge colour.
- `panel_title(title: Str, subtitle: Str, cx) -> Div`: 40 tall title row,
  Inter 14 SemiBold plus a muted 12 subtitle (empty for none) and a
  spacer; append 24 px `icon_button`s as children.
- `section_header(text: Str, cx) -> Div`: 30 tall, 12 SemiBold `text`;
  append `badge(..)`, a muted suffix, `spacer()`, buttons.
- `spacer() -> Div`: `flex_1`, pushes later children right.
- `list_row(id: Id, selected: bool, cx) -> Stateful<Div>`: 24 tall row chip
  inset 6, radius 6, solid hover, `pill` + Medium when selected; override
  with `.h(..)` / `.mx(..)` (22 in debugger columns, 26 in menus, 32 in the
  palette with `.rounded(px(8.))`).
- `overlay(cx) -> Div`: palette and dialogs (`panel_raised`,
  `border_strong`, radius 12, `elevated_lg`).
- `popover(cx) -> Div`: popovers and menus (radius 10, padding 4,
  `elevated`).
- `input_frame(focused: bool, on_raised: bool, cx) -> Div`: 28 tall input
  frame, `border_strong`, `accent` edge when focused, `canvas` fill on
  raised surfaces.
- `border_width(el, px) -> el`: fractional border width on any `Styled`.

### Marks (`components/marks.rs`)

- `ember() -> EmberMark` with `.size(px)`, `.color(hsla)`: the 7 px
  `accent` diamond.
- `unsaved_ring(color: Option<Hsla>, cx) -> Div`: the 8 px hollow ring,
  1.5 px `accent_alt` (or `color`) stroke.
- `status_dot(color) -> Div`: a 7 px dot.
- `logo(size: Pixels) -> Logo`: the spark (arc in `text`, diamond in
  `accent`); 22 in the title bar, 56 on the welcome screen.
  `logo_spark_box(size)` gives the diamond's box.

### Pill tabs (`components/pill_tab.rs`, `components/tab_layout.rs`)

- `pill_tab(id: Id, label: Str) -> PillTab`, then `.icon(IconName,
  Option<Hsla>)`, `.active(bool)`, `.focused(bool)` (ember on the active
  tab), `.dirty(bool)`, `.ring_color(hsla)`, `.closable(bool)`,
  `.badge(text: Str, Option<Hsla>)`, `.size(TabSize::{Editor, Panel,
  Session})` (6.6 / 6.8), with shorthands `.panel()` (bottom panel tabs:
  icon 14, padding 0 9) and `.compact()` (24 tall terminal sessions,
  label 12, icon 13, active fill `hover`), `.italic(bool)`, `.strikethrough(bool)`, `.label_color(hsla)`,
  `.max_label_width(px)`, `.tooltip(Str)`, `.on_click(Click)`,
  `.on_close(Click)` (stops propagation, implies closable).
- `PillTab::build(self, cx) -> Stateful<Div>` to chain more handlers
  (middle click, drag); otherwise use it as an element.
- `tab_trailing(active, dirty, closable) -> TabTrailing` is the rule for
  the trailing slot (ring, `x`, `x` on hover, nothing).
- `TabSize::metrics(&TypeScale) -> TabMetrics { height, text, icon, pad }`
  gives a size's numbers, for strips that line other controls up with it.

### Readouts, badges, kbd

- `readout(text: Str) -> Readout` with `.color(hsla)`, `.size(px)`: mono 12
  muted (`Ln 15, Col 18`, `exit 101 · 0.92 s`).
- `badge(text: Str) -> Badge` with `.color(hsla)`, `.fill(hsla)`,
  `.rail()`: 16 tall mono 11 count badge on `pill`; `.rail()` is the 14
  tall outlined rail badge.
- `kbd(keys: Str) -> Kbd`: one chip per key (`"Ctrl+Shift+P"`, chords
  split on spaces). `kbd_keys(label) -> Vec<Vec<String>>` is the split.

### Controls

- `button(id: Id, label: Str) -> Button` with `.style(ButtonStyle::
  {Primary, Secondary, Ghost})`, `.icon(IconName)`, `.icon_color(hsla)`
  (idle Run: `accent` play icon on a Secondary button), `.compact()` (26
  tall), `.disabled(bool)`, `.tooltip(Str)`, `.on_click(Click)`. Radius 8,
  padding 0 11, Inter 13 Medium, icon 15. Secondary is an outline.
  Disabled is solid, never opacity: `text_disabled`, an outline drops to
  `border`, a Primary trades its accent for a `pill` fill.
- `icon_button(id: Id, IconName) -> IconButton` with `.size(px)` (28
  default), `.icon_size(px)`, `.icon_color(hsla)`, `.active(bool)` (`pill`
  fill, `accent` icon), `.tooltip(Str)`, `.on_click(Click)`. Radius 7.
  `icon_size_for(size)` gives 16 / 15 / 14 for 28 / 26 / 24.
- `switch(id: Id, on: bool) -> Switch` with `.on_click(Click)`,
  `.tooltip(Str)`: 30x18 per 6.13.
- `segmented(id: &str, labels: &[&str], selected: usize, select: impl
  Fn(usize, &mut Window, &mut App), cx) -> Div`.
- `stepper(id: &str, value: Str, change: impl Fn(i32, &mut Window, &mut
  App), cx) -> Div` and `stepped(value, delta, step, min, max) -> f32`.
  (Moved from `settings_ui/widgets.rs`, which re-exports them.)
- `Tooltip::text(text: Str, cx) -> AnyView`: `panel_raised`,
  `border_strong`, radius 6, Inter 12, `elevated`.
- `divider()` / `vertical_divider()`: 1 px `border` hairlines.
